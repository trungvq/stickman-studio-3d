Viewing angle: a CHEATED FRONT VIEW, not a three-quarter view — do not rotate the piece in space and do not add perspective. The silhouette stays frontal and symmetric: both sides the same width, nothing on the far side foreshortened, narrowed or hidden. The CENTRE LINE is shifted to the viewer's RIGHT by about a tenth of the piece's width — the button placket, zip, tie, collar opening, the seam or parting that runs down the middle; it is the shifted centre line that makes the character read as facing right. Anything that sticks out FORWARD — a cap peak, a bill, the front of a brim, a knot, a nose — is drawn in side view, pointing to the viewer's RIGHT, its whole shape visible. Every piece on the sheet is turned the same way: never mirror one of them, facing the other direction is done later by flipping the whole character.
